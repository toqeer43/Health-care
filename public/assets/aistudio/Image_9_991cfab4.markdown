## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of biometric precision and vibrant, life-affirming vitality. Designed for a high-performance wellness audience—ranging from everyday health optimizers to competitive athletes—the aesthetic prioritizes immediate cognitive clarity, energetic momentum, and technological authority.

The visual direction draws from **Modern Clean Dashboard & Refined Precision UI**:
- **Fresh & Dynamic:** Energetic botanical tones balanced against grounded slate backdrops to evoke organic health powered by clinical computational intelligence.
- **Data-Dense Yet Breathable:** High data density mediated through generous whitespace, high-contrast typography, and purposeful surface layering.
- **Polished Tactility:** Crisp, micro-bordered surface containers with subtle luminescence rather than heavy skeuomorphism, prioritizing biometric graphs, macronutrient wheels, and dynamic activity rings.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for a dark-first environment that emphasizes vital biometric telemetry and minimizes eye strain during dawn-to-dusk tracking routines.

- **Primary Emerald (`#10B981`):** Represents core vitality, target completion, and positive metabolic health. Used for primary calls-to-action, success states, and key metric progress arcs.
- **Secondary Teal (`#14B8A6`):** Symbolizes hydration, oxygenation, and steady-state recovery. Applied to secondary trackers, respiratory graphs, and supplemental badges.
- **Tertiary Citrus Lime (`#84CC16`):** Adds kinetic energy and active exertion cues. Reserved for high-intensity bursts, caloric expenditure, and critical motivational highlights.
- **Neutral Slate Foundation (`#0F172A`):** Anchors the interface with deep, cool-toned darks (`#020617` for base canvas, `#0F172A` for primary containers, and `#1E293B` for elevated cards), ensuring pure accessibility and rich contrast for health metrics.
- **Functional Semantics:** Soft coral red (`#F43F5E`) for caloric surplus or elevated alerts, amber (`#F59E0B`) for nutritional deficits or warnings, and electric indigo (`#6366F1`) for sleep phase analytics.

## Typography

The type system blends the energetic geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the structural precision of **Inter**.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans with tighter tracking to produce authoritative, athletic dashboard summaries and meal/workout titles.
- **Biometric Metric Values:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans Bold with strict proportional alignment to ensure macro numbers, calorie counts, and metabolic trends read instantly.
- **Body & Continuous Text:** Handled by Inter to guarantee clean legibility across AI meal breakdowns, ingredient descriptions, and health insights.
- **Labels & Micro-Data:** Set in Inter semi-bold to bold with deliberate tracking expansion (`+0.04em` to `+0.06em`) for chart labels, daily targets, and status chips.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive grid structures dashboard views, while a unified 8pt grid token rhythm governs intra-component geometry.

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column fluid grid, 24px gutters, 32px canvas margins. Maximum canvas content constrained to 1440px for comfortable panoramic scanning of metric rings and weekly breakdowns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, 20px gutters, 24px margins. Cards reflow into 2-column macro pairs; secondary telemetry slides below the primary pulse module.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, 16px gutters, 16px canvas margin. Cards stack vertically. The primary nutrition ring centers horizontally with horizontal snap-scrolling carousels for meals and micro-trackers.
- **Component Density:** Dense data visualizations apply `space-xs` and `space-sm` internal padding, while analytical dashboard cards use `space-lg` to create separation between visual metrics and supporting numerical tables.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal layer stacking with ambient emerald illumination** and fine hairline borders, avoiding thick opaque dropshadows.

- **Base Layer (L0):** `#020617` (Deep Obsidian Canvas).
- **Surface Level 1 (L1 - Primary Containers):** `#0F172A` paired with a crisp 1px perimeter border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (L2 - Interactive Cards):** `#1E293B` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` outline. On hover, the border transitions to `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)` with an ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (L3 - Overlays & Modals):** `#1E293B` reinforced with a 16px backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`, and a depth shadow: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Glow Accents:** Key progress indicators (such as a 100% completed nutrient ring or an optimal glycemic score) emit a concentrated radial underglow (`filter: drop-shadow(0 0 12px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.45))`).

## Shapes

The interface uses a **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry to balance technical dashboard structure with human wellness friendliness.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** 0.5rem (8px) radius. Ensures crisp alignment in table rows, inputs, and form controls.
- **Cards & Visual Containers (`rounded-lg`):** 1rem (16px) radius. Softens biometric modules, meal cards, and trend graphs while maintaining structural discipline.
- **Major Dashboards & Modal Panels (`rounded-xl`):** 1.5rem (24px) radius. Used for hero overview containers and floating diet logging sheets.
- **Pills & Data Rings:** Full rounded radius (`9999px`) reserved for biometric status pills, macro distribution tags, search inputs, and radial SVG progress tracks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Emerald `#10B981` background, `#020617` text (Plus Jakarta Sans Bold), 8px border-radius, `0.75rem 1.25rem` padding. Subtle top-edge inset highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`). Hover initiates a scale to `1.02` with an emerald glow.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent base, 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), `#F8FAFC` text. Hover shifts background to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` and border to `#14B8A6`.
- **Ghost Action:** No border, `#94A3B8` text, hover shifts to `#10B981` with `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)` background fill.

### Cards & Telemetry Containers
- Built on `#0F172A` with a 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` border and 16px radius.
- Cards maintain distinct header sections with title-md typography, paired with right-aligned action chips or secondary icon buttons.

### Chips & Badges
- **Nutrient Indicators (Protein, Carbs, Fats):** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- Semi-transparent fills (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`) paired with solid high-contrast text (`#10B981`) and matching 1px border (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`).

### Inputs & Food Search Fields
- 8px radius, `#020617` background with 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` border.
- Floating label or crisp inside placeholder text in `#64748B`.
- Focus state activates an emerald border `#10B981` with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)` ring. Leading icons for voice AI analysis or barcode scanning.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- **Checkboxes:** 4px radius, `#1E293B` border; selected state fills with `#10B981` displaying a crisp white `#FFFFFF` checkmark.
- **Toggle Switches:** 20px pill track with 16px round thumb. Inactive: `#334155` track. Active: `#10B981` track with `#020617` thumb and soft glow.

### Lists & Meal Logs
- Borderless table/list rows separated by 1px divider lines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`).
- Left-aligned icon or meal thumbnail (rounded 8px), followed by title and ingredient breakdown in `body-sm` (`#94A3B8`), ending with right-aligned bold caloric values.

### Specialized Health Components
- **Pulse Macro Rings:** Multi-track concentric SVG rings displaying Protein (Emerald), Carbs (Teal), and Fats (Citrus Lime). Track backgrounds sit at 15% opacity; active tracks use smooth rounded caps with drop-shadow glows at terminal heads.
- **AI Recommendation Callout:** Card featuring a subtle left-to-right gradient border from `#10B981` to `#14B8A6`, with a micro Sparkle/AI icon and high-legibility advice text in `body-md`.
